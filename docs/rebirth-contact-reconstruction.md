# Source-preserving SM5 reconstruction prototype

2026-08-31. This is an **offline prototype**, not an installed fix for Cloud's arm or a validated preset. The ray algorithm remains the pinned Rebirth source. No production quality gate was relaxed.

Newer integration: [SharedQuad native implementation](rebirth-contact-shared-integration.md) reuses the same donor result using audited group memory rather than recomputing neighbors. This document retains the reference behavior and its motion/captured-resource tradeoffs; shared execution does not resolve those quality findings.

## What is reused

`src/ThirdParty/ShaderInjector/RebirthContactReconstruction.hlsl` is generated by `tools/import_rebirth_contact_source.py`. Its checkerboard parity and AVG reconstruction statements are copied unchanged from donor caller lines 1907 and 1930. Provenance includes their exact text, hashes and source lines; the MIT notice is retained. SM6 `QuadReadLaneAt` plumbing is replaced with explicit scalar lane values, not a different averaging algorithm.

`RebirthContactReconstructedKernel_ps.hlsl` is the Remake prototype. It invokes the existing guarded receiver reconstruction for each required pixel. Selected pixels trace their own ray; unselected pixels recompute the two selected neighbors in their absolute-screen 2x2 quad. Each neighbor loads its own depth, normal and packed material and uses the same native light. Uncovered/invalid neighbors remain neutral. Strength is applied after donor averaging. The native per-light contribution gate and physical diffuse/specular lanes remain intact.

This reference avoids sharing arbitrary compute lanes or introducing barriers inside varying native material branches. The subsequent native audit found the outer light-loop boundary itself is group-uniform, enabling the newer shared implementation. Neither approach reproduces pixel-shader raster/helper coverage. The donor's early-sky cutoff is also retained in the raw Remake adapter. The optional prototypes do not change the runtime stager's selection.

## Explicit compatibility and cost limits

- Phase is provisionally native `asint(cb1[139].z)`, the native eight-phase sampling input. The captured value 5 agrees with row 147.x in `(5,8,0.06476139,0.3672809)`, which resembles temporal-AA sample data. That is not frame-sequence proof that it matches donor `TemporalAAParams.x` across resets, cuts or different TAA settings. The adapter does not read guessed row 147 as a verified binding.
- Absolute screen quads are used, not viewport-relative or native thread-index quads. Viewport-edge safety behavior may differ from raster helper invocations.
- In the fully eligible case, recomputation averages 1.5 ray evaluations per pixel versus the donor's 0.5 with shared results. Extra receiver validation also costs work. This is not a performance-equivalent implementation.
- General native candidate size grows from the previous raw candidate's 66,336 bytes to 214,008 bytes; temporary-register count grows from 53 to 56 (39 native). Those are compiled-code measurements, **not measured GPU cost**.
- The later game temporal AA and complete native tiled dispatch are not reproduced by these tests.

## Matched motion result: tradeoff, not a pass

Both current audits use 16-sample donor rays, animated donor IGN, pixel-center depth and generated neighboring normals, 96 frames per scene, and identical geometry. Final repeated geometry also repeats its noise frame. The CPU truth still follows continuous surface points, so pixel sampling can disagree near silhouettes. Neither test runs engine animation or temporal AA.

| Moving-box diagnostic | Raw pixel rays | Donor quad reconstruction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Active receiver samples | 43,855 | 43,855 |
| False-hit samples | 207 | 276 |
| Missed eligible blockers (of 2,787) | 282 | 351 |
| Large changes with unchanged binary shadow truth | 1,159 | 745 |

Artifacts: `artifacts/rebirth-contact-pixel-motion-20260831-v2` and `artifacts/rebirth-contact-quad-motion-20260831-v2`. Both have zero invalid outputs, exact repeated endpoints, and no false hits on the unobstructed plane, sphere or perturbed/quantized sphere scenes. Both remain `regressionDetected=true`. Reconstruction reduces large changes but increases false/missed classifications. Do not call it a motion fix or suppress these failures to install it.

Earlier tracked-point raw audits are historical and not the matched baseline above. Reconstruction test v1 stopped on fixture-only signed division/modulus warnings; the fixture now uses unsigned division and modulus. Donor statements were not altered to silence warnings. Current parameterized production builds retain strict warnings.

### Pixel-center truth diagnostic

`tools/analyze_rebirth_pixel_truth.py` reuses the known analytic plane/box scene to check whether tracked-point truth is misleading at the actual selected pixel. It reads the original GPU readbacks, verifies their CSV agreement, and reproduces **all 49,152 recorded moving-box geometric truth rows** before comparing pixel centers and selected neighbors. Five independent analytic invariants pass in `tools/test_rebirth_pixel_truth.py`. Receipt: `artifacts/rebirth-contact-pixel-truth-20260831-v2/analysis.json`.

| Diagnostic (original active receiver mask retained) | RawPixel | RecomputeQuad |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original false-hit samples | 207 | 276 |
| Those with a geometric shadow at the selected pixel center | 3 | 2 |
| Those still unshadowed at the pixel center | 204 | 274 |
| Those with unshadowed center **and** both selected neighbors | 188 | 251 |
| Original missed blockers still eligible at pixel center | 276 of 282 | 344 of 351 |
| Large changes coinciding with changed pixel-center truth | 11 of 1,159 | 3 of 745 |

Only 24 of 43,855 active samples change binary geometric truth when moving from the tracked point to the pixel center. Thus receiver discretization is not a sufficient explanation for most recorded failures. A shadowed selected neighbor could explain some reconstruction spread, but does not establish acceptable quality. The next useful diagnostic is the donor ray's sampled depth and accepted intersection, including its bias/thickness behavior, without silently altering donor statements.

Limits: analytic moving-box geometry only, no FF7 diagnosis; ideal pixel-surface truth is not a biased-ray or TAA oracle. CPU float32 projection can differ near exact pixel boundaries: 163 active samples are flagged within 0.001 pixel of a boundary, including one raw and two reconstructed original false hits. Original results and deployment gates are unchanged. RawPixel traces every receiver; checkerboard labels in its diagnostic are comparison metadata, not skipped rays. The analysis checks the relevant scene/shader source fingerprints; it does not turn the older motion receipt into a current full-runner build receipt.

## Matched captured-resource replay

Completed `artifacts/rebirth-contact-capture-raw-20260831-v1`, `artifacts/rebirth-contact-capture-quad-20260831-v1`, and `artifacts/rebirth-contact-capture-comparison-20260831-v1`. Both paths bind the same saved Remake cb0/cb1/cb4, packed normal, device depth and **legacy linear BGRA8 material texture at t2**. The first variant's saved resource hashes are verified before/after replay; source and readback hashes are retained. The game is untouched.

The older regular stride-8 replay would sample only even/even pixels, all traced at captured phase 5, never exercising reconstruction. These donor replays instead sample complete absolute-screen 2x2 quads on the stride-8 lattice: 960x540 outputs, 518,400 samples per light, half traced and half reconstructed. Five lights each run enabled and disabled: **10,368,000 visibility outputs across both paths**, all finite/in-range and all disabled values exactly 1. Receiver-preflight flags are exported separately; they are not native light-contribution or hit masks.

Independent comparison reads the raw rays and reconstructs each quad on the CPU, including invalid-center neutrality. Across 2,592,000 enabled samples the maximum difference is 2.3841858e-7; selected/traced pixels are bit-identical for all five lights. The pinned donor source importer still passes. This verifies reconstruction identity, not shadow correctness. The saved noise index is 61 and provisional phase is 5; one frame does not verify phase progression/reset.

| Light index | Light-facing proxy samples | Raw shadowed | Reconstructed shadowed | Neutral samples acquiring >1% shadow | Shadowed samples returning to neutral |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 50 | 301,865 | 24,827 | 28,235 | 4,251 | 851 |
| 38 | 10,613 | 3,207 | 3,369 | 249 | 87 |
| 54 | 86,330 | 4,692 | 4,957 | 517 | 252 |
| 20 | 31,714 | 1,317 | 1,410 | 112 | 19 |
| 52 | 14,589 | 2,199 | 2,514 | 437 | 122 |

The proxy reuses the existing captured tile-index decoding, normal facing, radius and spot attenuation calculations. It omits native shadow maps, IES, BRDF and actual indirect dispatch coverage; it does not prove visible final contribution. Shadowed means visibility <0.999; acquisition/return use 0.99/0.999 thresholds to avoid numerical noise. Counts are per-light, not unique screen pixels, and acquisition/return are not false-hit labels without geometric truth.

The sample contains 2,110 material-ID-7 pixels. For light 50, 642 fall within the proxy, and shadowed counts increase 120 to 142; for light 54, 1,947 fall within it, increasing 113 to 138. Some changes cross material boundaries, but most measured changes are within the default-lit ID 1. Hair-ID mapping remains structurally corroborated rather than visually confirmed. Thus this capture does not justify dismissing the earlier synthetic motion regressions as only a hair/material issue, nor does it prove those regressions appear in the live scene.

No quality gate changed. The distinct donor replay receipt/layout does not satisfy the old production software gate, and neither prototype is deployed.
